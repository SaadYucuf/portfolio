Furniture manufacturing / Manufacturing ERP

Diran

A furniture manufacturing project where the full production workflow was systematized and supported with Face ID integration for operational control.

Discuss a similar projectView all projects

Key processes

Production planning, furniture orders, workshop operations, warehouse movement, process control and Face ID-based attendance/access integration.

Role

Business analysis, AS-IS / TO-BE process modelling, production workflow systematization and technical coordination for Face ID integration.

Business value

Manufacturing operations became more structured, easier to monitor and better connected to internal control through one digital workflow.

Case study context

The Diran project focused on turning the furniture production cycle into a clearer operating system: from incoming orders and production stages to warehouse movement, internal process control and Face ID integration. The goal was not just to digitize screens, but to make the manufacturing flow easier to track, coordinate and improve.
